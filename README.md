# Hifz Planner

A free, calm daily planner for **Quran memorization (hifz)**: 30 minutes a day.

**Open the app:** https://hifzplanner.github.io/hifz-planner/

## How it works
Tick the surahs and ayat you already know, choose your pace and rest day, and the app builds your plan. Each day it shows:

- **New lesson** (about 12 min): about the length of Surah 2, Ayah 4. Longer ayat are split over several days.
- **Recent revision** (about 8 min): your last 7 lessons.
- **Old revision** (about 10 min): a rotating cycle through everything you know.

## Features
- Read the Arabic text and listen to the lesson with repeat (1, 3, 5 or 10 times)
- Choose the order: easy and important first, from Al-Baqarah forward, or from An-Nas backward
- Set your own start date, rest day, pace and revision time
- Estimated finish date, streak, progress and a surah map
- Restart the journey at any time and keep your starting selection
- Works on iPhone and Android: add it to your home screen
- Light and dark mode

## Privacy
No account and no tracking. Your progress is saved only in your own browser. Use **Settings → Export backup** to keep a copy.
